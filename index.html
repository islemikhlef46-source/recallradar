Active recall, not re-reading

Re-reading feels productive — it isn't. RecallRadar forces you to retrieve information, the study method with the strongest evidence behind it. Answers in, gaps out.

A radar for your gaps

Every answer feeds the radar. It maps which concepts you keep missing and how badly — so revision time goes where it matters, not where it feels safe.

Revenge rounds

Finished a quiz? The radar rebuilds a fresh round aimed only at what you got wrong — different questions, same weak spots — until the radar goes clear.

Credits & disclosures

RecallRadar is a single-file web application. Everything in it — the interface, the question engine, the radar artwork and the capybara — is original, hand-built for this project.

  • No external libraries, frameworks, fonts or icon packs are used.
  • Zero network requests — the app runs entirely offline in your browser.
  • Notes pasted into the app never leave your device.
  • Built with vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
